## Lessons from episode 1
- Silhouettes in front of black houses disappear: build streets as a corridor with the light in the gap.
- Long titles ran off the frame: `Title` now auto-fits to 940 px.
- Spinning puppets got paper-thin at the turn: scale X never below 0.4.
- A dark saint "shadow" in #1A0E14 was invisible on the dark backdrop: use #3A1C2A-#3A2340.
- Labels under doors collided with captions: keep notes out of y 1440-1560.
