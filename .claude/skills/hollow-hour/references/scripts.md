# Script rules (Short, 60-70 s)

- 190-230 words. Jack at natural pace: 211 words = 81.6 s raw; pauses capped at 0.38 s + atempo 1.1 = 67.8 s.
- **Hook (first 2 sentences):** a date, a place and one strange action, said flat: "In July 1518, a woman stepped
  into a street in Strasbourg and started to dance." Then a short, cold second beat ("Nobody knew why. And she
  didn't stop."). No "Did you know", no "this will haunt you".
- **Escalation:** each 8-10 s adds a stranger turn (others join -> the doctors prescribe more dancing -> people
  collapse). Short fragments as punches ("Hot blood.").
- **Honesty lines are part of the creepiness:** "Later accounts say...", "Historians still argue about those
  numbers." Never state a disputed number as fact.
- **Answer near the end:** the best explanation in plain words (Waller: famine, disease, belief in a cursing saint).
- **Loop line:** the last sentence returns to the first ("It all began with one woman, in a street in Strasbourg, in
  July 1518.") and the last shot matches frame 0.
- Write years for the voice as words ("fifteen eighteen"); captions merge them back to digits.
- Deliver: one-sentence idea, voice-over, word count (`wc -w`), fact-check sheet with links.
