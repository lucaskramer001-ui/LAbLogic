Privacy Policy

Lab Logic Publisher · Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. What the tool accesses

When the Lab Logic account owner connects TikTok, the tool receives the permissions user.info.basic (basic profile information such as display name and avatar) and video.publish (to upload and publish Lab Logic's videos). For Instagram and YouTube it receives the equivalent upload permissions for Lab Logic's own accounts.

2. How it is used

The data is used only to confirm which account is connected and to post Lab Logic's own videos to it. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

3. Storage

Access credentials are stored as protected settings of the tool and are never published. No data about other users is collected.

4. Removing access

The account owner can remove the tool's access at any time in the platform's app settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services). Stored credentials are then deleted.

5. Contact

Through the Lab Logic channel pages on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok.
