Stuck on a raft, surrounded by ocean: why don't we just drink seawater? The problem is the salt.

Your kidneys have to flush the salt out in urine, but they can't make urine as salty as seawater. So getting rid of the salt from one cup takes more water than that cup gave you, and every sip leaves you thirstier.
