Voyager 1 is 49 years old, one light-day from Earth, running on less than half its power and down to two working instruments. So why don't we just turn Voyager 1 off? The answer is the whole Voyager story: a planet line-up that comes about once every 175 years, a launch with about 3.5 seconds of fuel to spare, volcanoes on Io, the Pale Blue Dot, and repairs made from a full day away.
