# Voyager long-form: production plan (draft for approval)

**Working title:** Why Don't We Just Turn Voyager 1 Off?
**Format:** YouTube long-form, 1920x1080, 60 fps, about 9 min (about 1,350 words).
**Style:** Lab Logic cartoon (scientist, objects with faces, ink outlines) mixed with real NASA photos on Polaroid cards,
as in the 1977 Short.
**Status:** plan only. Nothing has been generated or spent yet.

## 1. The idea in one sentence
Voyager 1 is 49 years old, one light-day away and down to its last instruments. So why don't we just switch it off? The
answer is the whole story: a once-in-175-years shortcut, 3.5 seconds of fuel, volcanoes on Io, the Pale Blue Dot, and
repairs made from a day away.

The "Why don't we just...?" frame keeps the channel's format and gives the story one question to pay off at the end.
A plain history video would only be a timeline.

**Opening 0:00-0:35 = hook A, then the turn:** "It's forty-nine years old, it's running on less than half its
power, and it has two working instruments left. So why don't we just switch it off? To answer that, you need the
whole story. And it starts with planets lining up."

**On screen from frame 0:** the scientist types "HELLO?" on a chunky cartoon phone and presses send. A pulse leaves
Earth, and a corner timer starts: **"REPLY IN 23:59:59"**. This running gag pays off in the last 20 seconds, when the
reply finally arrives.

## 3. Retention devices (used throughout)
1. **"Voyager's lives" HUD:** a heart bar in the top-right corner, like a game display but with our own design. Each
   near-death costs a heart with a crack and an SFX, then the repair refills it. That gives five near-deaths and five
   open loops.
2. **Re-hook at every chapter end:** for example, "That was the first time Voyager almost died. It wasn't the last."
   / "The next problem wasn't fuel. It was a single capacitor."
3. **Distance and age counters** at the bottom left, ticking up at each chapter jump (km, light-time and year).
4. **Pattern change every 20-40 s:** cut, zoom, a real photo, a diagram, a scientist reaction, or a scale gag.
5. **Payoff:** the HELLO reply lands at the end, and the question "why not switch it off?" is answered in chapter 9.
6. **No intro, no subscribe pitch, no "before we start".** One ask at the very end, pointing to the next video
   (end screen).

## 8. Decisions for the user before production
1. **Length:** about 9 min. The skill's rule is 5-7 min, but a story needs room, and retention devices hold it.
   Alternative: a tight 7-min cut that drops chapters 4 and 8.
2. **Real photos of people** (Carl Sagan, Linda Morabito, JPL mission control in 1979): use archival NASA photos?
   The brand rule says the scientist is the only cartoon human. Documentary photos would be new for the channel.
   Default: no photos of people, names only.
3. **Music bed:** a quiet ambient track would help a 9-min video. It needs an ElevenLabs connector with music, or
   the YouTube Audio Library added by the user. Default: SFX only, as before.
