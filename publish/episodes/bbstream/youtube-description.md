Why don't planes just stream their black box data live? In 2014 an airliner vanished and its black boxes were never found, yet you can text from 35,000 feet. Streaming everything is possible, but it costs a fortune and almost all of it would never be needed.

▶ The full black box story (Air France 447 found 3,900 m down, MH370, why the box is orange): https://youtu.be/RTJLi1DO8S0

An industry estimate from 2002 put the cost for one big US airline at about $300 million a year. In 2024 there was about one accident for every 880,000 flights. Pilots also object to broadcasting cockpit audio. So aviation chose a middle ground: since 2018 airliners report their position at least every 15 minutes, and newer big jets must send their position every minute when they're in distress. The black box stays, because it still works when every signal is gone.
