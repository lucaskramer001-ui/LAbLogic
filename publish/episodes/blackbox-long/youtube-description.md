If the black box survives a plane crash, why don't we just build the whole plane out of it? Because the black box isn't a material: it's a few memory chips inside thick insulation and a titanium or steel shell, tested to survive 3,400 g, an hour at 1,100 °C and the pressure 6 km under the sea.

Armor a whole airliner like that and it could never take off, and no one inside could survive 3,400 g anyway. What actually keeps people alive is different: crash-tested seats, 90-second evacuations, and the lessons investigators read from the recorders.
