In July 1518 a woman began to dance in a street in Strasbourg, and within weeks somewhere between 50 and 400 people were dancing with her. This is the dancing plague of 1518.
The city's cure was more dancing. Historian John Waller's explanation is famine, disease and fear of a saint believed to curse people with dancing.
