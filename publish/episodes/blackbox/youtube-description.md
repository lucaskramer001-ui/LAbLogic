If the black box survives a plane crash, why don't we just build the whole plane out of it? Because the black box isn't one magic material: it's a few memory chips wrapped in insulation and a titanium or steel shell.

That armor is tested to survive a 3,400 g impact, a 1,100 °C fire for an hour and being crushed under more than two tonnes. Armor a whole airliner like that and it could never take off, and nobody inside could survive 3,400 g anyway.
