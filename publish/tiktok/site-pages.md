## Page 3: Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy - Lab Logic Publisher**
Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. What the tool accesses. When the Lab Logic account owner connects TikTok, the tool receives the permissions
   user.info.basic (basic profile information such as display name and avatar) and video.publish (to upload and
   publish Lab Logic's videos).
2. How it is used. The data is used only to confirm which account is connected and to post Lab Logic's own videos
   to it. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
3. Storage. Access credentials are stored as protected environment settings of the tool and are never published.
   No data about other TikTok users is collected.
4. Deleting access. The account owner can remove the tool's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and
   privacy > Security > Apps and services (or Manage app permissions). Stored credentials are then deleted.
